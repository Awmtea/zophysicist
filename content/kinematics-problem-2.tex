\section*{Problem 2 --- Ball Thrown Upward}

A ball is thrown straight up with initial velocity
$u = 20\ \text{m/s}$. Take $g = 9.8\ \text{m/s}^{2}$ downward and ignore
air resistance.

\textbf{Find:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The maximum height the ball reaches.
  \item The total time in the air before returning to launch height.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Hint.} At the highest point the velocity is momentarily zero.
Choose upward as positive, so $a = -g = -9.8\ \text{m/s}^{2}$.
\end{quote}
