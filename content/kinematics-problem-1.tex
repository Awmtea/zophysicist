\section*{Problem 1 --- Accelerating Car}

A car starts \textbf{from rest} and accelerates uniformly at
$a = 2\ \text{m/s}^{2}$ for $t = 5\ \text{s}$ along a straight road.

\textbf{Find:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The final velocity of the car.
  \item The distance it travels in this time.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Given:} $u = 0\ \text{m/s}$, $a = 2\ \text{m/s}^{2}$,
$t = 5\ \text{s}$. Try it before opening the solution.
\end{quote}
