\section*{Solution --- Ball Thrown Upward}

Choose \textbf{upward as positive}. \quad
\textbf{Given:} $u = 20\ \text{m/s}$, $a = -g = -9.8\ \text{m/s}^{2}$.

\subsection*{Part 1 --- Maximum height}
At the top $v = 0$. Use the equation without $t$:
\begin{align}
  v^{2} &= u^{2} + 2as \\
  0     &= (20)^{2} + 2(-9.8)\,s \\
  s     &= \frac{400}{19.6} \approx 20.4\ \text{m}
\end{align}

\subsection*{Part 2 --- Total time of flight}
Time to reach the top ($v=0$):
\begin{equation}
  0 = 20 + (-9.8)\,t_{\text{up}}
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  t_{\text{up}} = \frac{20}{9.8} \approx 2.04\ \text{s}
\end{equation}
By symmetry the fall takes the same time, so
\begin{equation}
  t_{\text{total}} = 2\,t_{\text{up}} \approx 4.08\ \text{s}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Check}
Return velocity should be $-20\ \text{m/s}$:
\begin{equation}
  v = 20 + (-9.8)(4.08) \approx -20\ \text{m/s}\;\checkmark
\end{equation}
