\section*{Velocity}

\textbf{Velocity} is the rate at which displacement changes with time ---
informally, \emph{speed in a stated direction}.

Average velocity:
\begin{equation}
  \vec{v}_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\Delta \vec{x}}{\Delta t}
\end{equation}

Instantaneous velocity:
\begin{equation}
  \vec{v} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0}\frac{\Delta \vec{x}}{\Delta t}
          = \frac{d\vec{x}}{dt}
\end{equation}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Type:} vector
  \item \textbf{SI unit:} metres per second ($\text{m/s}$)
  \item \textbf{Symbol:} $v$ (or $\vec{v}$)
\end{itemize}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Example.} A car covering $100\ \text{m}$ north in $5\ \text{s}$
has an average velocity of $20\ \text{m/s}$ north.
\end{quote}
