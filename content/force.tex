\section*{Force}

A \textbf{force} is a push or a pull arising from an object's interaction
with another object. A net force can change an object's speed, direction
or shape.

\begin{equation}
  \vec{F} = m\vec{a}
\end{equation}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Type:} vector
  \item \textbf{SI unit:} newton (N), where
        $1\ \text{N} = 1\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}^{2}$
  \item \textbf{Symbol:} $F$ (or $\vec{F}$)
\end{itemize}

If the net force is zero, the object is in \textbf{equilibrium}: at rest
or moving at constant velocity.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Example.} A net force of $10\ \text{N}$ on a $2\ \text{kg}$ block
gives $a = F/m = 5\ \text{m/s}^{2}$.
\end{quote}
